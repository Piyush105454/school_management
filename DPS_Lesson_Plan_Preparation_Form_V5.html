Step 4 · Discussion & Participation

How will students discuss, answer and ask?

Plan one connected discussion instead of several separate question boxes.

💬
?
Keep the discussion simple and two-way.

Write the question you will ask, how students will respond, and how you will invite their questions.

Quick, inclusive and time-conscious support
Check every bench: Scan notebooks and responses across the room instead of staying with only a few students.
Support quickly: Give thinking time, an easy starting question, one correction or brief bench-partner support.
Protect learning time: Avoid long scolding, arguments or repeated correction while the rest of the class waits.
Red Zone follow-up: If a concern cannot be resolved quickly, give a calm direction, continue the lesson, mark it for follow-up and speak with the student separately after class.
One connected paragraph or a few short points are enough.
